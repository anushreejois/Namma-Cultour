1915 Heritage Landmark Cubbon Park
Primary Archive

State Central Library (Seshadri Iyer Memorial)

ARCHIVE ID: CUB-01 ENTRY: FREE FOR PUBLIC

Tucked inside Cubbon Park’s ancient bamboo grove, this crimson Tuscan-Corinthian landmark holds over 300,000 rare volumes, including Braille collections and centuries-old maps of the Mysore Kingdom.

Hours: 8:30 AM – 7:30 PM (Closed Mondays)
Atmosphere: Silent High-Ceiling Reading Hall
Trail Connection: Stop 1 of Cubbon Cultural Loop
